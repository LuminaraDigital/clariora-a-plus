LGA 1700 CPU Socket and Retention Lever

Land Grid Array (LGA) packages place contact pins directly on the motherboard socket rather than on the processor. Align the Pin 1 gold triangle indicator to avoid pin damage. The Zero Insertion Force (ZIF) lever secures the Integrated Heat Spreader.

Key Exam Takeaway

Modern Intel desktop processors and AMD AM5 utilize LGA sockets. Contrast with legacy AMD AM4 Pin Grid Array (PGA) where pins were soldered directly on the CPU.
