Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Clariora ("we", "our") is designed with an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture. We do not sell personal information or track users across external platforms.

2. Data We Collect

  • Telegram User Profile: When opened inside Telegram, we receive basic identifiers provided by Telegram (User ID, First Name, Username) to associate study streaks and entitlements.
  • Local Study Progress: Quiz scores, flashcard mastery, and time logs are saved locally on your device or in Telegram CloudStorage.
  • Blockchain Interactions: If you connect a TON wallet to mint an on-chain credential, public transaction data is broadcast to the decentralized TON network as requested by you.

3. Telegram Stars & Payments

All in-app purchases for digital features are conducted via Telegram Stars (XTR). We do not store or process credit card numbers or financial account information.
